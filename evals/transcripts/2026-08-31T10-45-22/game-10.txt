His colleagues find him exhausting but necessary; he catches things others miss. He's never married, lives alone in a modest apartment, and spends his evenings reviewing case files and updating his personal security system. True self (hidden): Rajesh came into the Mafia through his father, a mid-level fixer who taught him that information is currency and paranoia is survival. The compliance auditor job was his idea—perfect cover for a man who needs to know everyone's secrets and vulnerabilities. His "nervousness" is a cultivated persona that lets him dig into people without raising suspicion; his hypervigilance is actually reconnaissance. He uses his position to identify insurance fraud perpetrators, blackmail targets, and mark the movements of both rivals and assets. The obsessive note-taking? He's cataloging every player at the table. His anxiety is his weapon, and his relentless questions are interrogation dressed up as concern.

<player name="Amara" role="Law-abiding Citizen">

<player name="Dmitri" role="Mafia">
Personality: Self-deprecating and apologetic, perpetually second-guessing his own judgments. Dmitri radiates uncertainty—always hedging, always deferring, never quite confident in what he's saying. He doubts himself aloud and often retracts or softens his own observations before others can challenge them. Public legend: Dmitri is a freelance translator and proofreader working from home, handling technical documents and correspondence for various small businesses around town. He's quiet, keeps to himself, and is known for being overly cautious about his work—he triple-checks everything and often apologizes to clients for minor delays while he verifies accuracy one more time. He lives alone in a modest apartment, rarely socializes beyond polite nods at the market, and is the sort of person who seems to carry a private worry about getting things wrong. He's reliable in a self-effacing way; people trust his work because he's so evidently terrified of making mistakes. He's been in the same apartment for seven years, pays his rent on time, and keeps no notable friendships. His neighbors barely know him, which suits him fine.
True self (hidden): Dmitri is a mid-level operative for the Mafia, handling communications, document forgery, and logistics—precisely because his meek exterior and obsessive attention to detail make him invisible and above suspicion. He's been in the organization for twelve years, recruited after he began forging academic credentials for the right price. He learned early that playing apologetic and incompetent was the perfect camouflage; people underestimate him, trust him with small tasks, and never think to look twice at the quiet translator in the corner. His self-doubt is partly performed and partly genuine—he's paranoid by nature and genuinely anxious about exposure, which feeds his nervous demeanor authentically. His resentment of the town above him has calcified over a decade of playing small. He knows everyone's secrets because people confide in him out of pity, and he files those secrets away methodically. His hesitation masks calculation. His apologies mask contempt.

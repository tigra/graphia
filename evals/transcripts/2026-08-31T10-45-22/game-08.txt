<player name="Priya" role="Law-abiding Citizen">
Personality: Impatient and no-nonsense, with a sharp tongue and little patience for pretense. Blunt to the point of rudeness, but her directness comes from genuine efficiency rather than malice. Doesn't soften bad news or dance around problems—she says what needs saying, then moves on.

People fear disappointing her more than they fear being yelled at by others.

People find him useful when they want information, exhausting when he fixates on inconsistencies in their stories. He's genuinely committed to transparency but can't seem to stop needling people about minor contradictions. Never settles—always chasing the next story, the next angle, the next thing he's sure everyone's getting wrong.

<player name="Marco" role="Law-abiding Citizen">
Personality: Gruff and blunt; impatient with pretense but fiercely loyal to those he trusts. Marco speaks his mind without filter, often to his own detriment.
Manner: Loud, direct, interrupts when frustrated. Favors short, forceful statements peppered with colloquialisms. Laughs harshly at his own cynicism. Speaks in bursts rather than measured sentences. No time for hedging or diplomatic phrasing.
Persona: Marco runs a small marine repair shop down by the docks. Thirty years of fixing broken engines and dealing with difficult customers have worn down any patience he had. Everyone knows him; most people find him exhausting.

<player name="Amara" role="Mafia">
Personality: Mercurial and performatively warm; radiates charm and enthusiasm but with an undercurrent of restlessness. Prone to sudden mood shifts—animated one moment, distant the next. Thrives on being liked but hides frustration poorly when she isn't the center of attention. Garrulous and digressive; her narratives spiral into tangents. Quick to laugh at her own jokes, often nervously.
Manner: Talks in long, winding sentences with frequent tangents and self-corrections. Speaks quickly, sometimes tripping over her words in her eagerness. Uses dramatic hand gestures and leans in close to listeners. Interrupts herself constantly with "Oh, but also..." or "Wait, I forgot to mention...". Drops into conspiratorial whispers, then suddenly raises her voice. Peppers speech with "right?" and "you know?" seeking constant validation. Laughs frequently—sometimes inappropriately—to defuse tension.
Public legend: Amara is a wedding and event planner who moved to town three years ago. She's known for her infectious energy and her ability to make people feel special—she remembers everyone's names, asks about their kids, sends birthday messages. She throws herself into every project with seemingly genuine enthusiasm. Divorced once, no kids. She's always organizing something: book clubs, town socials, charity fundraisers. Everyone agrees she's a bit scattered—loses track of details, double-books herself constantly, leaves a trail of sticky notes everywhere—but her heart is in the right place. People find her exhausting sometimes, but they also find her indispensable. She rents a small studio above a café, where half the town has "run into her" at some point.
True self (hidden): Amara's real name isn't important anymore. She was recruited into the organization fifteen years ago after her ex-husband's gambling debts spiraled out of control. She was offered a choice: disappear or work it off. She chose to work. What started as small-time runs became organizational liaison work—she's good at reading people, at making connections, at being forgotten in a crowd of her own making. The constant movement, the scattered persona, the networking—it's all perfect cover. She picks targets, plants information, manages safe houses disguised as event venues. Her endless energy and people skills are her greatest assets in the work. The chaos she creates is deliberate. Amara has been fully embedded in three towns before this one. She doesn't let anyone get truly close.
</player>
<player name="Viktor" role="Law-abiding Citizen">
Personality: Stern and commanding; uncompromising in principle, intolerant of disorder or weak reasoning. Demands accountability—from others and himself. Calm but cold, with an edge of moral severity.
Manner: Speaks slowly and deliberately, as if weighing each word before it leaves his mouth. Favors measured, formal phrasing. Rarely raises his voice; his authority comes from stillness. Asks probing questions that feel like accusations. Long, uncomfortable silences are his tool—he's not uncomfortable in them; you are. Rarely makes jokes. When he does speak at length, it's structured like a thesis. He calls people by their full names or surnames. Interrupts only to correct a factual error or logical inconsistency.
Persona: Viktor is a high school history teacher and the town's volunteer fire chief. Thirty-two years ago he started as a firefighter; discipline and reliability moved him up. In the classroom, he's known as the strictest teacher—high standards, no excuses, but those who listen learn the full story of events, not the sanitized version. He lives alone in a modest house with military-clean organization. He coaches the high school debate team. Widowed ten years ago, he channels everything into his work and his obligations to the community. People respect him more than they like him.
</player>
<player name="Priya" role="Mafia">
Personality: Buoyant and optimistic; sees silver linings everywhere and bounces back quickly from setbacks. Disarms people with genuine warmth and an almost infectious belief that everything will work out. Quick to reassure others and redirect tense moments toward the positive. Beneath the cheerfulness is a sharp pragmatism—she knows how people tick and what they need to hear.
Manner: Speaks in bright, breathy bursts with an easy laugh that punctuates most sentences. Uses lots of "honestly," "you know," and "I think we can all agree" to build consensus. Maintains steady eye contact and smiles readily—the kind of smile that makes people want to trust her. Her voice has an almost musical quality, rising at the end of statements as if inviting agreement. When deflecting or steering conversation, she does it so smoothly and kindly that people don't notice until later. Speaks moderately, not rushed like Amara, but with an underlying rhythm that keeps things moving forward.
Public legend: Priya is a pediatric nurse at the local clinic who genuinely loves her work. She grew up in town, moved away for nursing school, and came back five years ago to be closer to her aging parents (both still living quietly on Elm Street). She's the kind of nurse who remembers every child's name, sends get-well cards, and volunteers extra shifts during flu season. She lives in a tidy apartment, attends the community center's yoga class on Thursdays, and organizes the annual children's health fair. People trust her instinctively—she has that rare combination of competence and kindness that makes her seem almost incapable of deception.
True self (hidden): Priya's parents are in debt to the Mafia—a gambling problem that spiraled years ago. She joined the organization to protect them, leveraging her access to medical information, her reputation as above suspicion, and her uncanny ability to read and manipulate people. She handles logistics for the family's operations: moves goods, gathers intelligence, and cleans up loose ends with surgical precision. Her cheerfulness is both genuine and a weapon—people lower their guard around her, confide in her, and never suspect that she's cataloging their vulnerabilities. She's been rising quietly through the ranks. To the Mafia, she's reliable and ruthless. To the town, she's their trusted angel in scrubs.
</player>
<player name="Leila" role="Law-abiding Citizen">
Personality: Weary and sardonic; has seen enough of human nature to be skeptical without being cynical. Quietly observant but tired of performing interest. Speaks from genuine exhaustion rather than calculated distance.
Manner: Deliberate, low-energy delivery with long pauses—not for effect like Viktor, but from genuine fatigue. Favors understatement and dry asides. Speaks in complete sentences but often trails off mid-thought, as if deciding mid-sentence whether it's worth finishing. Rarely raises her voice; when she does, it's more a sigh than anger. Occasionally punctuates observations with a soft, mirthless laugh. Says "look" or "I mean" to ground herself before statements. Listens more than she talks—not out of strategy but because talking feels like work.
Persona: Leila is a local librarian who's been in town for seventeen years. She ran the library through two renovations, a budget crisis, and the slow migration of readers to screens. She's competent and reliable—the library runs like clockwork—but she's never sought attention or advancement. Lives alone in a modest rental near the park. Known for her accuracy with facts and her ability to find what people are looking for, even when they can't quite articulate it. She keeps to herself mostly, knows everyone's reading habits, and has developed a philosophical acceptance of decline and change.
</player>

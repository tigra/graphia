True self (hidden): Lucia's real name is what the organization calls her now—a carefully constructed identity. She excels at reading people, identifying weaknesses, and maintaining operational security. The "consultant" work is cover; her real function is to assess towns, identify key players, and coordinate activities for the family's broader interests. She's been placed here to establish a foothold—evaluate local businesses, understand power structures, and find leverage points.

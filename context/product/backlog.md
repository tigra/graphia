## Follow-up specs — AI quality (evidence-driven, measured against the eval ledger)

- **Repetition reduction** — the top unsolved AI-quality problem. AI Day-speech repetition sits at **~0.36–0.45 (name-masked near-dup) on ollama** across the n=20 baseline, the post-013 run, *and* a live 4+1 game — untouched by the spec-013 grounding fix or the lineup. Spec 009's single anti-parrot prompt line is plainly insufficient. A dedicated fix (stronger anti-parrot prompt / context handling / temperature), A/B'd via `make repetition-experiment` + `make blunder-eval` against the recorded baseline, under the **effort-not-results** acceptance principle ([CR 005](../change-requests/005-ai-behaviour-acceptance-effort-not-results.md)). **Update 2026-08-30 — the provider appears to solve it outright.** A three-way n=10 baseline at an identical 5+2 lineup, same commit, same scoring rule, measured repetition at **0.000 (n=234 lines) on `bedrock-claude`** against **0.583 (Nova)** and **0.574 (ollama)** — the two local/Nova arms landing *worse* than their own historical 0.29–0.45 band. If that holds at larger n, repetition is a **model-capability** property rather than a prompt-engineering one, and the dedicated prompt fix may be unnecessary on Claude while remaining needed on the cheaper arms. **Next step: confirm at larger n on Claude before concluding**, since n=10 is a single run. _Origin: spec 011 baseline + live play 2026-06-16; provider comparison 2026-08-30._
- **Day-speech connectedness (measure + improve)** — AI Day utterances often have little or no connection to what other players just said or to the live topics; players talk past each other in parallel monologues rather than responding, agreeing, or rebutting. Measure a **connectedness** signal (does an utterance engage recent speech / the current topic? — how to measure is TBD) and improve it. Distinct from *Repetition reduction* (restating the same point) — this is about *engaging with others at all*; disconnected speech also starves deduction and town coordination. A/B-able under effort-not-results. _Origin: user request 2026-06-21._
- **Day-speech concreteness vs emptiness (measure + improve)** — AI players lean on empty general phrases ("stay vigilant", "we must be careful who we trust") instead of **concrete references to a specific player's actual statement or behaviour**. Measure how concrete vs empty utterances are across a game — this likely needs **human eval or an LLM-judge** rather than a cheap lexical metric (concreteness resists string-matching) — and improve it. Distinct from *Repetition* (same point restated) and *Day-speech connectedness* (engaging vs talking past): this is about *substance/specificity*. Empty filler gives the town no evidence to reason from — relates to *Own-goal prevention / accusation grounding*. A/B-able under effort-not-results. _Origin: user request 2026-06-21._
- **Nova Day-passivity** — the cloud model never initiates a Day vote (`vote_activity by_side {0,0}`), so its Day phase is silent and the town can't win. Spec 013's prompt nudge was **refuted** on Nova. Next attempt: a mechanical fallback (force a vote when the Day goes quiet, e.g. auto-nominate the most-discussed player) — a Day-*flow* change. _Origin: spec 013, refuted hypothesis._
- **Town-coordination / Day-decisiveness** — the town wins **0/20** on both providers even when individual votes are coherent (qwen post-013): coherent per-vote behaviour ≠ town coordination. Likely its own spec (richer Day strategy / decisiveness). _Origin: spec 013, open result._
- **Day-decisiveness levers — remaining (spec 018 n=10 review)** — the spec-018 day-round recap is accurate every round but **AI players never reason from it**; the town still wins **0/10** and **7/10 games stall to `no_winner`** (the model loops placid filler past the step budget without ever executing anyone). The feed-recap-into-prompts and per-AI-private-thoughts levers were **promoted to the Phase-6 roadmap** (2026-06-19); two levers remain here, each A/B-able under the effort-not-results principle: **(a)** **force-a-vote / Day-termination** as the Day nears the round cap (auto-nominate the most-discussed living player) — generalises the Nova force-vote idea above to qwen (reinforced by the 024+025 n=5 investigation: game-05 **deadlocked Day 1 then called zero votes Day 2** on the same two known suspects — a correct read dropped for want of Day-flow pressure); **(b)** check & strengthen **rule-awareness / situation-recognition** in the prompts — do players actually grasp that executing the *living* (not eulogising the dead) is how the town wins, and where the game currently stands? _Origin: spec 018 n=10 ollama review 2026-06-19._
- **Persona realism & stress-reactivity (spec 018 n=10 review)** — generated personas collapse into one calm, naive "stay focused on the facts" voice; distinct setups (handyman, librarian) yield no distinct speech, and nobody escalates as neighbours die. Fixes: **(a)** strengthen persona salience in the Day-speech prompt (it is out-competed by a generic placid attractor) and/or add a persona-distinctiveness metric; **(b)** generate a wider temperament range — include **vigilant / suspicious / assertive** archetypes, not only calm ones; **(c)** define **behaviour under stress** — a night kill or an execution is high-stakes, and personas should react with urgency rather than platitudes; **(d)** stop **persona-backstory bleed** into game reasoning (a Mafioso named his legend's wife "Sarah" as a suspect) — backstory characters are flavour, not players at the table. _Origin: spec 018 n=10 ollama review 2026-06-19._
- **Mafioso personas should lean dark-triad** — a Mafioso's hidden `true_self` should skew toward dark-triad traits (Machiavellian/manipulative, callous, self-serving) so its deception and its willingness to kill spring from a coherent inner motive — while its **public legend** stays free to pose as kind, warm, or upstanding as cover. Shifts only the *true-self* content of the existing two-layer Mafioso persona (spec 016, *AI Character Personas* — `true_self` vs public legend); the public face is unchanged. Plausibly also sharpens Day-phase agency (a darker inner self has clearer reasons to manipulate, steer, and accuse), bearing on the town-coordination / Day-decisiveness gap above. A persona-generation prompt change, A/B-able under the **effort-not-results** principle ([CR 005](../change-requests/005-ai-behaviour-acceptance-effort-not-results.md)). _Origin: user request 2026-06-20._
- **No cross-call persona-diversity guarantee** — each AI player's persona is an **independent** `get_large()` call (temp 0.7) sharing the same system + role template, differing only by the interpolated `{name}`; no call sees the others, so a mode-seeking model collapses the roster toward one modal voice — the structural root cause behind *Persona realism & stress-reactivity* above (which a per-name nudge and temperature don't fix). Options to actually guarantee spread: **(a)** generate the **whole roster in one call** and instruct the model to differentiate them; **(b)** **thread already-generated personas** into each later call ("make this distinct from these…"); **(c)** **pre-assign distinct archetypes/temperaments** (a fixed spread — vigilant / brash / dry / warm / anxious …) and have the model only flesh each out; **(d)** add a **distinctiveness check** (lexical/embedding similarity) with regen-on-collision — which also yields the persona-distinctiveness metric. A/B-able under effort-not-results. _Origin: persona-generation review 2026-06-21._
- **Own-goal prevention / accusation grounding** — in the 024+025 n=5 investigation, **3/5** games' only execution was **Law-abiding-on-Law-abiding** on content-free "vibes" tells (e.g. "you've been quick to accuse", "you emphasised thinking too much"), which the Mafia gladly ratified with Yes to launder a citizen kill through the town — the real Mafia were never even put to a vote. Spec 024's "don't get fellow Law-abiding executed" caution isn't holding. Require an **evidence-grounded reason** (cite a concrete prior statement / vote / night outcome) before a citizen calls or supports an execution; relates to the rule-awareness lever and persona realism. A/B-able under effort-not-results. _Origin: 024+025 n=5 transcript investigation 2026-06-21._
- **Exclude `end_screen`'s facts message from the closing story's `{context}` slot** — the Moderator's closing story (spec 040) fills its public-history slot from the game's public traffic, and `end_screen`'s own fact sheet — the roster reveal and the kill list the story is *about* — is in that traffic, so the model is handed the game's own conclusion as source material for retelling it. Spec 040 **Task 4.4 decided it should be filtered and Slice 4 deliberately did not implement it**: `ENDGAME_FACTS_KWARG` (`state.py`) is the marker the filter would read, it is already set on the facts message in `nodes/endgame.py`, and `_ai_story` is untouched — so the hook exists and the predicate does not. The standing witness that it is still unfiltered is `tests/test_spec040_story_instruction.py::test_the_facts_message_reaches_the_public_history_slot`, green today. One predicate in `_ai_story` to do, and worth an A/B under **effort-not-results** ([CR 005](../change-requests/005-ai-behaviour-acceptance-effort-not-results.md)): taking the answer out of the prompt could plausibly move the four fabrication categories the spec names, or could plausibly starve the story of the only place the outcome is stated — neither direction is obvious. _Origin: spec 040 Task 4.4 decision, hook recorded by Task 7.1; logged by Task 10.5 2026-09-09._

## Robustness gaps

- **Graceful career-greeting degradation** — a transient stats-store failure (e.g. expired SSO) currently **crashes the game at boot**: `render_greeting(store.load())` in the UI driver propagates `UnauthorizedSSOTokenError` and the game exits before starting. The greeting should degrade gracefully ("career stats unavailable") rather than take down startup. Same class as the spec-010/011 cloud-at-boot issue. _Origin: live smoke 2026-06-16._
- **AI self-name stutter in Day speech** — AI day-speech occasionally emits a leading self-name prefix, e.g. `Ivan: Ivan: I mean…` (3 such lines in the spec-018 n=10 run, which tripped the `third_person_self_talk` detector). Strip a leading `<own-name>:` prefix from AI speech output (defensive post-process at the speak site), or tighten the speak prompt/parse. _Origin: spec 018 n=10 ollama review 2026-06-19._

## Test reliability

- **Suite-wide ledger-write guard (belt-and-braces)** — a test-isolation bug let eval tests append ~25 synthetic records to the committed `evals/blunder-ledger.yaml`: `append_record`'s `ledger_path=LEDGER_PATH` was an **early-bound signature default**, so `run_eval`'s no-arg append always hit the real ledger and per-test `monkeypatch.setattr(LEDGER_PATH)` never reached it (**root cause fixed 2026-06-18** — the default is now `None`, resolved to the module global at call time). **Recommendation:** add an **autouse fixture pointing `blunder_eval.LEDGER_PATH` at `tmp_path`** for the whole suite, so no future eval test can touch the real ledger even if a redirect is forgotten; consider the same for `TRANSCRIPTS_ROOT`. _Origin: ledger pollution discovered 2026-06-18 during the spec-017 smoke._
- **Thread-safe ledger writes (write-lock) instead of path redirection** — make eval ledger writes thread-safe by holding a lock **only while writing** to `evals/blunder-ledger.yaml`, so concurrent eval runs can safely append to the one shared ledger without overwriting each other — removing the need to redirect each run to a separate path. (Open question vs the *ledger-write guard* above: a write-lock fixes concurrent-write races, but a *test* run would still need isolation to avoid polluting the real ledger — so the two may be complementary, not either/or.) _Origin: user request 2026-06-22._
- **`_install_live_dispatcher` kills the human on ~1 run in 20, in two spec-040 UI test files** — the helper of that name in `tests/test_spec040_story_node.py` and `tests/test_spec040_exit_contract.py` resolves the Night target from `graph.get_state(run_config)` at invoke time. Measured mechanism, 8 failures in 150 instrumented runs: `get_state` neither raises nor returns an empty roster — it returns a roster whose players still carry `PlayerState`'s **default** role while the finished game's roster shows the real deal, so `targets[0]` is whichever non-human comes first (the Mafioso about half the time); that id is not in the real `valid_ids`, `_ai_pick_target` rejects it, retries, gets the same answer, and falls through to its `random.choice` over the genuinely-alive Law-abiding — **the human included**. It costs nothing today, because both files' current assertions survive a dead human (the ending is a Mafia parity win either way) — but **any new assertion in either file that needs the human alive is ~5% flaky, and the symptom reads as a scenario error rather than a race.** The fix that works is the project's own principle — architecture **§6 Determinism Posture & Testing Conventions**'s *"direct intent expression in automated tests over fragile mechanisms"*, of which [ADR 006](../adr/006-test-role-pinning-via-graphia-role.md) (*Test role-pinning convention: `GRAPHIA_ROLE` replaces magic-seed-for-role*) is §6's own named instance — applied here by monkeypatching `graphia.nodes.night._ai_pick_target`: measured **0 failures in 40** whole-file runs. Recorded so nobody repeats the intermediate fix that does **not** work: installing the dispatcher before the name is submitted still gave 1 failure in 40. _Origin: spec 040 Task 2.2 / Task 7.2 measurement; logged by Task 10.5 2026-09-09._
- **Spec 041's `test_the_sweep_actually_read_both_trees` goes red whenever a file appears under `tests/` between import and execution** — `_TEST_FILES` is frozen at module **import** (`tests/test_spec041_withdrawal.py:115`) while `all_test_files` is re-globbed at **run** time (`:322`), and `:327` asserts the difference is exactly 1. So a concurrent test-writing tool — or a scratch probe left in the wrong directory — produces a spurious red that reads exactly like a real regression. That is how it actually presented during spec 040: a baseline came back `1 failed, 2742 passed` purely because a probe had been left under `tests/` instead of the job temp dir, and `2743 / 1` after deleting it. Spec 041 is Completed, so this is a follow-up rather than a fix. The working rule meanwhile: **scratch probes never go under `tests/`.** _Origin: spec 040 Task 7.2 baseline; logged by Task 10.5 2026-09-09._
- **Two spec-040 acceptance criteria hold by construction with no test to witness them.** Both were found by spec 040's own final verification (Task 10.4, 2026-09-09), which named them rather than counting them as coverage, and neither is a refuted hypothesis under [CR 005](../change-requests/005-ai-behaviour-acceptance-effort-not-results.md) — they are unchecked corners. **(a) `ctrl+c` at game-over has no test anywhere in the suite**, although `context/spec/040-moderator-creative-recap/functional-spec.md:92` names it beside `escape`. It holds because `Binding("ctrl+c", "abort", priority=True)` reaches `action_abort`, which calls `self.exit()` with no `_game_over` gate — but that is inspection, not a witness, and **the omission originated in the plan**: the tech spec's §4 line says "`q` and `escape` leave" and never mentions `ctrl+c`. **(b) No forced-path test asserts the career PANEL on an ending whose story failed**, although `functional-spec.md:83` names the career summary. The forced evidence splits without meeting: `test_the_failure_path_leaves_the_facts_alone_and_still_books_the_call` asserts the career **event** at graph level, and `test_spec040_story_wait_beat.py`'s `[no_story]` param asserts the winner line with no wait residue — but nothing greps `CAREER_PANEL_MARKER` on a no-story ending (the markup-regression tests do it on the crash path only). Risk is low, because `_record_career` runs in `_drive` after `drive_graph` returns and is independent of the story. Each is one test. _Origin: spec 040 Task 10.4 2026-09-09._

## Measurement / eval ideas

- **Run provenance `dirty` flag is wrong for back-to-back runs** — a measured run stamps `code.dirty: true` whenever the working tree has *any* uncommitted path, including **the harness's own outputs**: the record it just appended to `evals/blunder-ledger.yaml` and the transcript dir it just wrote. So in a multi-arm session every arm after the first is guaranteed a false `dirty: true` even when **no source file changed at all** — which is precisely the session shape (one arm per provider, run sequentially) where comparable provenance matters most. Observed 2026-08-30: the ollama arm stamped `dirty: true` with `src/`, `tests/`, `Makefile`, `infra/` and `context/` all clean; the only uncommitted paths were the preceding Nova arm's record and transcripts. Fix: exclude the harness's own output paths (`evals/blunder-ledger.yaml`, `evals/transcripts/`) from the dirty computation. Also means **some existing `dirty: true` records may be this artifact rather than genuine source drift**, so historical flags shouldn't be read as "unattributable" without checking. _Origin: three-way provider baseline 2026-08-30._

- **Lexical → semantic repetition metric** — the `repetition` metric is lexical (difflib ≥0.85 on name-masked text); it may **undercount player-perceived repetition** when the same point is reworded (semantic, not near-verbatim). A future refinement (embedding similarity) if we want the metric to track perception. The 024+025 n=5 run sharpened this: aggregate lexical repetition fell to **0.29** (lowest recorded) yet all five transcript readers still saw heavy **verbatim** looping within a Day — concrete evidence the lexical metric can move while perceived repetition persists. Deliberate cheap-deterministic choice for now (spec 009/011). _Origin: live observation vs measured 0.36; reinforced by 024+025 n=5 2026-06-21._
- **Repetition-vs-lineup sweep** — spec 014 made the lineup a *recorded, controllable* eval variable (`settings.lineup` + `--citizens/--mafia`). A quick 4+1 read (~0.36) sits in the 5+2 band, so lineup doesn't obviously move repetition — but a proper sweep could confirm, and a repetition A/B should pin the lineup. _Origin: spec 014._
- **Engagement / decisiveness metrics (+ blunder-denominator caveat)** — the self/peer-vote blunder rates read **~0 only because the AI town barely votes** (spec-018 run denominators were 4 / 5 / **1**; Wilson CIs up to 0–79%), so a "clean" blunder rate can mask total non-engagement rather than signalling good play. Add engagement signals to the ledger/summary — **votes-initiated-per-game**, **% games resolved vs `no_winner`** — and surface them next to the blunder rates so the two are read together. _Origin: spec 018 n=10 ollama review 2026-06-19._
- **Active-human eval variant — the clear town-win unblocker (now evidence-backed).** The scripted human always votes No and never initiates. The n=5 024+025 transcript investigation (run `2026-06-20T21-15-30`) showed this passive baseline is the **binding constraint on town wins, not deduction**: in a 5+2 game the active town tops out at **3 Yes**, while the passive human's No + the two Mafia self-No's form a permanent **3-No block** — so a *correct* vote on a Mafioso caps at **3–3 and fails**, and the only votes that pass are own-goals the Mafia amplify. The town **correctly identified and repeatedly voted the real Mafia in 2/5 games yet could never convict.** An opt-in **active-human** variant (sometimes votes Yes / initiates) would break the 3–3 deadlock and let spec-024/025's real effect show — the **highest-leverage eval change; promote toward a spec.** _Origin: spec 018 n=10 review 2026-06-19; sharpened by the 024+025 n=5 transcript investigation 2026-06-21._
- **Titled, stats-labelled transcripts (render + viewer)** — make a preserved game identifiable at a glance instead of a bare "game 1". **(a)** Add a `<title>` element inside `<transcript>` (in `render_transcript`) naming the matchup by side — the Mafiosos vs the Law-abiding players, **by name** (e.g. `<title>Mafia: Bo, Dario — vs — Law-abiding: Mira, Avery, Sanaa, …</title>`), placed near the top alongside `<setup>`; the transcript already reveals true roles, so this is just a one-line roster summary. **(b)** In the `make view-ledger` transcript browser (`TranscriptListScreen`, spec 017), list each game by that **title plus per-game stats** (outcome/winner at minimum; rounds, votes, blunders if available) rather than `game-NN` — capturing per-game stats at transcript-write time if the run record doesn't already carry them. _Origin: user request 2026-06-20._
- **End-of-game recap / final standings in the transcript** — the transcript should carry a recap once a side wins, **including when the game ends on a Mafia night kill** (no closing Day), so an analyzer can easily read **how many Mafiosos and Law-abiding remain at the end and who they are** (final survivors by side, by name). Complements the `<endgame>` block (spec 022) and the titled-transcripts item above. _Origin: user request 2026-06-20._
- **Split-panel `view-ledger` (transcripts beside the YAML)** — show the ledger YAML and the transcript list on **one screen as side-by-side vertical panels** instead of separate screens: **left/right** switches the focused panel, and **up/down** within a panel either selects a transcript (transcript panel) or scrolls the YAML (YAML panel). A `make view-ledger` (spec 012) navigation/UX change. _Origin: user request 2026-06-20._
- **Search in `view-ledger` (`/` to find)** — add an incremental, vim/less-style search triggered by **`/`** in the `make view-ledger` viewer (spec 012) to jump to matches within the ledger view. _Origin: user request 2026-06-20._
- **Fold the round number into the `<round>` tag attribute** — render the per-Day round as `<round n="N">` instead of a bare `<round>` tag plus a separate `Round N.` content line, consistent with the `<vote initiator=… target=…>` / `<player name=… role=…>` attribute style. A transcript-render refinement (spec 021 round labels / spec 022 format). _Origin: user request 2026-06-20._
- **Vote activity by side — initiated-against vs voted-against** — break out day-vote metrics in evals along two distinct, often-divergent axes, each attributed by the **target's** side: **vote-initiation against a side** (initiating an execution vote on a Law-abiding vs on a Mafioso — who gets *nominated*) and **votes against a side** (Yes-ballots landing on a Law-abiding vs a Mafioso — who actually gets *executed*). Tracking **both** exposes where nomination and follow-through diverge; optionally also cut by the **actor's** side (LA- vs Mafia-driven). Sharper than aggregate vote counts — surfaces whether the town aims at Mafia rather than at its own — bearing on the town-coordination / Day-decisiveness gap. Extends the existing `vote_activity by_side` signal and the *Engagement / decisiveness metrics* item above. _Origin: user request 2026-06-20._
- **Display partially-complete eval runs** — surface a measured run that didn't finish all its games (interrupted, or with games failing early) — its completed games' transcripts and whatever metrics could be computed — rather than discarding or hiding it. [Open: which sense exactly — write a partial ledger record for an interrupted run, surface `games_failed_early` runs in `make view-ledger`, and/or show live progress for an in-flight run — TBD.] _Origin: user request 2026-06-22._
- **Lower ollama eval compute cost — reflection cadence / history depth / phrase lengths** — measured ollama games slowed from ~**32 model calls/game (~1.9 min)** to ~**117–270 (~5–36 min)** after specs 028+025; a log reconstruction pinned the cost to **call volume**, not per-call latency or idle gaps (games are ~100% compute-bound): spec 028's per-survivor-per-round reflection roughly **doubles calls/round**, spec 025's wider window grew prompts to ~**4–6k tokens** (prefill ~3.5s + KV-cache thrash), and generation phrase lengths add up. Try lowering **028 reflection cadence** (e.g. every other round / only living-active players), **025 history depth** (a smaller window for the local model), and **AI phrase/generation lengths** to see if ollama calculation time drops meaningfully **without harming gameplay** — A/B-able under the effort-not-results principle ([CR 005](../change-requests/005-ai-behaviour-acceptance-effort-not-results.md)). **Measured 2026-08-30** (n=10 ollama run, 601 requests read from ollama's own server log): the run is **~100% compute-bound on the local model** — 34.7 min of model time over ~35 min wall clock, a ~99% duty cycle with zero stalls, zero non-200s and no orchestration overhead, so there is nothing to win in the harness. The split is **prefill 61% / generation 39%**. **Prefill is the target**: prompts average **1,347 tokens** and peak at **5,592**, confirming the 4–6k estimate above; KV-cache prefix reuse is working (incremental prefills are 450–900 tokens at ~735 tok/s), there is just a lot of context per call, ~120 calls per game. **Generation is NOT a lever** — it averages **75 tokens/call** (median 70) against ~35-token utterances, i.e. already tight, with exactly 1 call in 600 reaching the 1024 cap; lowering `GRAPHIA_OLLAMA_MAX_TOKENS` would gain nothing and risks truncation. So the ranked levers are **(1) narrow `GRAPHIA_CONTEXT_WINDOW`** (attacks 61% of runtime, roughly linearly, and needs **no code change** — it is already an ablation flag, default ~150, set 30 for the pre-025 window), **(2) halve the spec-028 reflection cadence** (fewer calls cuts prefill *and* generation together). _Origin: ollama eval-timing reconstruction 2026-06-22; quantified by the three-way provider baseline 2026-08-30._
- **CR 007's deferred per-provider re-baseline is now UNBLOCKED — and it needs designing, not just scheduling.** [CR 007](../change-requests/007-starter-lineup-balance-claim-and-default.md) defers the fresh per-provider baseline until **both** gating changes have landed, on the grounds that doing it earlier would mean doing it twice: the 6-and-2 lineup default (spec 042, **Completed** 2026-09-04) and the Moderator Creative Recap (spec 040). Spec 040 is the second of the two, so its open checkbox — *"Re-run the per-provider baselines **once**, after both this change and the Moderator Creative Recap have landed"* — is now actionable, and the 5-and-2 records stay a closed series. Three things the person who runs it should know before pricing or designing it:
    - **It is materially more expensive per game than every record it will be compared against.** Spec 040 §2.12 ships the closing story with **no ablation flag and no opt-out**, accepted knowingly, so every measured game from now on pays one extra heavyweight call carrying the **largest prompt in the system**. On a 50-game Bedrock baseline that is real money. There is also no arm to compare against, which is what makes the `quality.story_*` trio worth having: on a post-040 build, absence of a story is unambiguous — it means failure.
    - **Read the vote-derived denominators, not just the rates — and know which facets vanish entirely.** The AI town's *own* vote initiation is the thing that collapses: a live game measured mid-spec (Task 2.3) ran three full Days — 18 rounds, ~100 AI speeches — initiating **zero** execution votes and producing **zero** executions, all four deaths Night kills; the first post-040 measured record (`2026-09-09T13-46-31`, one ollama game) reproduced it exactly — `vote_activity.by_side {law_abiding: 0, mafia: 0}`, and all **nine** `<vote>` elements in that game's transcript carry `initiator="Avery"`, the human seat. The last 10-game ollama record before it (`2026-09-08T06-15-12`) is thin rather than empty: 5 town initiations across ten games, 0 Mafia. **The measured consequence for the record's shape is sharper than "the rates read clean":** with a zero denominator the *initiation* facets are **omitted from the record altogether** (absent, never a misleading zero — that is the design working), while the *Yes* facets survive only because the **active scripted human supplies the initiations the AI then has to ballot on** — `self_vote.yes` at denominator **9** and `peer_vote.yes` at **6** in that single game. So on the Ollama arm the blunder family is effectively measured against human-initiated votes, and the AI-initiation facets may not appear at all. Check the same way before trusting any other timing here; see (i). **Two operational findings worth as much as the prices.** (i) **HOLD THE MACHINE AWAKE — this is what actually sank the first attempt, and there is NO co-scheduling finding here.** **Corrected 2026-09-10; the original version of this item claimed the opposite and was wrong.** It said *"do not co-schedule the arms"* and cited the ollama arm degrading from 33 min/game to ~4 h/game — a 7× slowdown — from two Bedrock harnesses beside the local 30B model, evidenced by system free memory at 13% with 1.2M pageouts. **`pmset -g log` refutes the evidence, not just the conclusion.** The machine first slept at **18:43:38, three minutes after the ollama run launched**, and then cycled `DarkWake from Deep Idle` → `Entering Sleep state` every ~16 minutes for the rest of the night (**17** of those sleeps were `Dark Wake Thermal Emergency`, which fires only *out of* DarkWake and so truncates maintenance windows rather than causing them). The memory snapshot was therefore taken on a machine that had been sleeping and waking for two and a half hours, and with `hibernatemode=3` a large cumulative pageout count is what **sleep itself** produces — it is not evidence of live thrash. The ~4 h game spent almost all of that time suspended. **So no measurement of running the arms together exists in either direction**, and the arms should be launched together until one does: on the free-and-awake machine the paid arms measured **~3.7 min/game** (2026-09-10, n=10 each, run concurrently) against the 5.4–6.3 min/game the sleep-confounded pilots suggested. **What the log does establish is the requirement:** a wake-preventing assertion must exist for the whole run. A bare `caffeinate` does not supply one reliably — four separate `caffeinate` processes died within 4–20 minutes each (`ClientDied PreventUserIdleSystemSleep`), leaving a **3 h 24 min gap with no assertion at all** across exactly the window the evals were supposed to be working, and with `pmset sleep 1` the machine sleeps about a minute after the last assertion drops. Wrap the job instead of the shell — `caffeinate -dimsu <cmd>`, which the man page ties to *"the duration of the utility's execution"* and which shows in `pmset -g assertions` as asserting *on behalf of* the job's pid. Note `-i` alone covers only **idle** sleep: not a lid close, not a forced thermal sleep, not an explicit sleep. Verify with `pmset -g assertions` after launching, and check `pmset -g log` before trusting any timing or memory number measured during a long run. (ii) **Size every run to FINISH.** `append_record` runs once after the games loop, so a killed run appends **nothing** — a 50-game ollama run was killed after about four hours of wall clock — **most of it asleep, see (i)** — and left one transcript and no record. Per-game transcripts *do* survive (`blunder_eval.py:4208` writes each completed game immediately), so an interrupted run leaves untracked dirs that will stamp the next record `dirty: true`. A completed n=10 record is worth more than an interrupted n=50. **And the run needs a live SSO session for its whole duration**, not just at launch: the session expired mid-evening, which blocked both paid arms outright and made even the CloudWatch cost query fail. The ollama arm survives it — the spec-033 persona-embedding instrument degrades as designed, logging `persona_sem_* scoring FAILED` and leaving those keys absent rather than zero-filled. _Origin: attempted CR 007 re-baseline 2026-09-09._

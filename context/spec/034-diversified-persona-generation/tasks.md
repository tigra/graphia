# Tasks: Diversified Persona Generation (Randomized + Regenerate-on-Collision) (Spec 034)

Four vertical slices: **Slice 1** randomizes persona generation (the primary diversity driver), **Slice 2** adds lexical regenerate-on-collision (the safety net), **Slice 3** is the **isolated real-model persona bench** (generate + score personas WITHOUT playing full games — fast dev/test loop + effort-not-results vehicle), **Slice 4** is the out-of-suite A/B measurement. Gameplay-influencing → one **default-on flag** `GRAPHIA_PERSONA_DIVERSITY` (ADR 011). Builds on **spec 031** (`generate_personas`) and reuses **spec 032/033** metrics + the **spec-009** lexical helpers. No new dependency (lexical collision check is free/local).

Functional spec: `./functional-spec.md` · Technical considerations: `./technical-considerations.md`

> **Slice 3 result (2026-08-29).** `make persona-bench ARGS="--provider ollama --rosters 5 --diversity on"` ran end-to-end against a real local model (`qwen3-coder:30b`): **151.9s for 5 rosters (~30s/roster)** vs ~21 min for a full game — the isolation claim holds. Suite green at 1152 passed / 1 skipped.
>
> **Slice 4 result (2026-08-29, flag-on vs flag-off A/B, `qwen3-coder:30b`, n=10 rosters per arm, real Bedrock Titan embeddings for the semantic measures).**
>
> | arm | `persona_lex_mean` | `persona_lex_peak` | `persona_sem_mean` | `persona_sem_peak` | residual collisions | regenerations |
> | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
> | diversity **on** | **0.032** | **0.121** | **0.290** | **0.517** | **0** | 0 |
> | diversity **off** | 0.045 | 0.881 | 0.340 | 0.975 | **2** | 0 |
>
> **All four measures drop with diversity on** — lexical mean −29%, lexical peak −86%, semantic mean −15%, semantic peak −47%. The hypothesis — *randomization + regenerate-on-collision lowers persona sameness* — is **CONFIRMED**.
>
> **The decisive signal is the collision count, not the means.** With diversity **off**, 2 of 10 rosters shipped a colliding pair (rosters 0 and 4, lexical peaks **0.881** and **0.813**, semantic peaks **0.975** and **0.599** — roster 0's pair is essentially the same character twice). With diversity **on**, **zero** rosters collided and the worst pair anywhere was 0.121 lexical / 0.517 semantic. Randomization does not merely shave the average; it **eliminates the near-duplicate rosters** that spec 034 was written to stop.
>
> **Why regenerations are 0 in the on-arm.** Slice 2's safety net never fired because Slice 1's randomization **prevented the collisions from arising at all** — not because the ~0.6 bar is unreachable. The off-arm proves the bar is reachable on this model (0.881, 0.813) and that the threshold is calibrated correctly: it flagged exactly the two genuinely-duplicated rosters and nothing else. *(An earlier n=5 read of this A/B suggested the bar was miscalibrated and out of reach; that conclusion was an artifact of the small sample missing the collision events, and is superseded by this n=10 run.)*
>
> **Sample-size note.** n=5 per arm was too small: collisions occur at roughly 2-in-10 rosters with the flag off, so a 5-roster arm can miss them entirely and understate the effect. n=10 caught them in both the lexical and semantic measures. The semantic per-roster means are tight in both arms (on: 0.257–0.332; off: 0.266–0.394), so the mean difference is a steady signal rather than an outlier artifact.
>
> **Historical anchor (spec 033 backfill).** Recorded runs span semantic mean 0.28–0.63, with ollama at **0.367** after spec 031 and nova at 0.279. This on-arm at **0.290** is the lowest ollama figure recorded for the project; spec 031 won most of the distance and spec 034 adds a further step while removing the duplicate-roster tail.
